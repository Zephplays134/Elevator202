01Project overview

Project 2026 is the next game from Tableboard Studios, a Roblox studio making maritime simulators. It sits next to their live game, Cruise Ship Simulator (CSS), on the studio's official list of current games.

The studio hasn't revealed the official name, genre details or the feature list. What the public record shows: the project exists, it's labeled 2026, it's the lead developer's top current project, and there's a dedicated testing server plus a separate “building systems” development place.

07FAQ

Is Project 2026 being tested right now?

Yes. Internal testing is confirmed by the tracker owner, and a “Project 2026 Testing Server” experience is publicly listed under the lead developer. It's internal, so the public can't join.

When does it release?

Not announced. The official group lists it as “Project 2026 (2026) · To be announced.”

Can I join the testing?

No public test access has been announced. Watch the official group and the studio's Discord.

Will it have elevators?

Unknown. No public source mentions elevators for Project 2026 yet. This page will mark them confirmed only when the studio shows them.

Is it a sequel to Cruise Ship Simulator?

Unconfirmed. Both games are on the studio's current list, and CSS is still being updated (latest 26 Oct 2026). The studio hasn't said how they relate.

Is this an official Tableboard Studios site?

No. This is a fan-run tracker. Official info comes from the Roblox group, the studio's Discord and their social accounts.
