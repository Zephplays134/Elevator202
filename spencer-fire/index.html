The Shurfine Food Mart fire in the Village of Spencer, Tioga County, New York, on Friday, September 11, 2026. Every fact below comes from a published source listed at the bottom of the page.

Was it an accident?

NOT DETERMINED

The cause is under investigation. Officials have not called it accidental, and they have not called it arson.

Every report I could find says the origin and cause are being investigated by the Tioga County Fire Investigation Team. WETM reported on Sept. 14 that “the cause of the fire has not been determined.” The most recent coverage, from Oct. 10, 2026, does not report a finding.

04Damage & people

Building

A total loss (Star-Gazette). By Monday, Sept. 14, WETM reported that “the entire store is destroyed, leaving nothing but a pile of rubble.” An employee told The Ithaca Voice the fire “got up into the ceiling and it was gone,” and that the old timbers were “seasoned and dry.”

Injuries

Store employees evacuated safely and moved cars out of the parking lot to give crews room (Star-Gazette). On the day of the fire, WBNG said it was not yet known whether anyone was hurt. None of the reports I read mentions an injury. No official injury count found

Workers

One employee estimated 20 to 30 people worked there (The Ithaca Voice, Sept. 11). Later coverage described “nearly two dozen displaced employees” (The Ithaca Voice, Oct. 10).

Responders

Spencer FD was helped by Candor, Halsey Valley, Lockwood, Tioga Center, Waverly-Barton, Campville, Weltonville, Community Fire & Rescue, West Danby, Danby and Newfield. Bangs Ambulance, NYSEG, NYSDOT and Austin Excavating also assisted (Star-Gazette).

06Community impact

  • It was Spencer's only full-service grocery store. A Dollar General remains in the village. The nearest grocery stores are in Sayre, Owego, Watkins Glen or Ithaca (Star-Gazette; The Ithaca Voice).
  • A Spencer-Van Etten school district survey drew 140 responses. About 30 said getting food had become difficult or very difficult, and 18 were worried about having enough food over the next 30 days (The Ithaca Voice, Oct. 10).
  • The Spencer-Van Etten Food Cupboard, which serves about 220 to 240 families a month, used to buy meat and bakery items from the store (The Ithaca Voice, Oct. 10).
  • The Food Bank of the Southern Tier is working with local partners on food access (WBNG, Sept. 14).
  • A GoFundMe for displaced workers was organized by a former store manager and his wife. A benefit was scheduled for Oct. 18 at The Shoe Inn Bar and Grill in Van Etten (The Ithaca Voice, Oct. 10).

07FAQ

So was the fire an accident?

No one official has said so. As of the latest reporting I could find, the cause had not been determined and the Tioga County Fire Investigation Team was investigating. This page will say “accidental” only if investigators say it.

Was it arson?

There is no public report of arson, and no arrests or criminal charges have been reported. “Not determined” means just that: no conclusion has been made public.

Is this the Spencer's gift store?

No. This page is about the Shurfine grocery store in the Village of Spencer, New York. I found no news report of a fire at a Spencer's (Spencer Gifts) store in New York.

Where exactly is Spencer, NY?

It's a village in Tioga County, in New York's Southern Tier. The store was on North Main Street, a few blocks from Spencer-Van Etten Middle School.

Was anyone hurt?

Employees got out safely. None of the reports I read mentions an injury, but I did not find an official injury count.

Will the store reopen?

No reopening or rebuild has been announced. The village says it supports efforts to keep a grocery store in Spencer. Cleanup and demolition are waiting on permits.

How can people help?

According to The Ithaca Voice (Oct. 10), you can use the community food-sharing cabinets (for example, 160 N. Main St. in Spencer), the Spencer-Van Etten Community Food Cupboard (91 Orchard St.), and the GoFundMe for displaced employees.
